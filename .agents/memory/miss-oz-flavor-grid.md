---
name: Miss Oz flavor grid
description: Product expectations for adding ice cream flavors to the menu grid.
---

Append new flavors in the requested order and let the existing grid flow naturally. Do not reorder cards or center/resize an odd final row to balance the layout.

**Why:** The flavor count is expected to grow over time; a temporarily uneven final row is intentional.

**How to apply:** When adding flavors, use the existing card component and append to the catalog/photo data. Keep existing cards and grid styling unchanged unless the client asks otherwise.
