---
name: Miss Oz one-page staging
description: The approved boundary for visual cleanup before the site's later page restructure.
---

Keep Miss Oz as one page while improving its palette, typography, menu, and section structure. Do not create dedicated menu, about, wholesale, events, or new-flavor routes or remove major homepage sections until the final sitemap is approved.

**Why:** The feedback prefers useful visual and internal cleanup over committing to a multi-page architecture before the final page structure is settled. Invented images, products, prices, and business claims would make that handoff harder.

**How to apply:** Treat the homepage menu, seasonal flavor, voting, cheesecake order, wholesale inquiry, events, navigation, and footer as reusable sections. Retain the existing submission behavior, voting behavior, and external order links while restructuring. Use supplied real photos rather than generated substitutes.
