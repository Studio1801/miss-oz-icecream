name: Miss Oz one-page staging
description: The approved boundary for visual cleanup before the site's later page restructure.
---

Keep Miss Oz as one page while improving its palette, typography, menu, and section structure. Do not create dedicated menu, about, wholesale, events, or new-flavor routes or remove major homepage sections until the final sitemap is approved.

**Why:** The current feedback explicitly prefers useful visual and internal cleanup now over committing to a multi-page architecture before the final page structure is settled. Product and wholesale photos are to be supplied later; invented images, products, prices, and business claims would make that handoff harder.

**How to apply:** Treat the homepage menu, seasonal flavor, voting, cheesecake order, wholesale inquiry, events, navigation, and footer as reusable sections. Retain the existing submission behavior, voting behavior, and external order links while restructuring. Use supplied real photos once available rather than generated substitutes.
