name: Miss Oz page structure
description: The current scope for Miss Oz navigation and homepage sections.
---

Keep existing homepage content as scrolling sections, while Events, Wholesale, About Us, and Contact have dedicated pages. The Contact navigation item must always open its dedicated page, including from Home; retain the visit details on Home. The five primary navigation items are Home, Events, Wholesale, About Us, and Contact. Do not change the visual design, layout, fonts, colors, copy, or animations.

**Why:** The client originally requested only Events, Wholesale, and About Us as separate pages, but subsequently explicitly approved Contact always opening its own page because differing destinations depending on the starting page confuse visitors. Existing homepage content and design must remain unchanged.

**How to apply:** Keep the hero/carousel, menu and cake ordering, flavor voting, guestbook, and visit details on Home. Preserve the existing Events, Wholesale, About, and Contact content on their own routes. Contact navigation always targets /contact, never the Home visit anchor. Other section links may target home anchors from dedicated pages. Avoid inventing teasers or changing layout, animation, design, typography, color, or copy unless the client explicitly asks.
